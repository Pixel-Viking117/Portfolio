Introduction


My name is Chris, and I am a solo indie developer. As an AuADHD neurodivergent creator, I leverage my unique traits to my advantage.

My ADHD allows me to tap into deep hyperfocus, fueling out-of-the-box thinking and innovative ideas. To balance this, I utilize structured timers and reminders to keep my projects securely on track.

My autism provides a strong affinity for routine and optimized workflows. I excel at building structured pipelines, whether I am creating personal assets or developing for a company. I love bringing ideas from reality into the virtual world.
